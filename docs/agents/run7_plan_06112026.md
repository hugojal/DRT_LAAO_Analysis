## A. Decisions for the user (they change the geometry or the BC)

### A1. Re-cut the PV extensions (review C2-1, 0.15–0.25 points)
**Finding.** The review found two PV caps pointing about 82° away from their vein axis, with cylinders 1.5–2× the lumen diameter.

**Rough check here.** PCA of the LA mask near the end of each vein, against the cap normal:

| cap | angle to vein axis |
|---|---|
| 1 | 73–77° (axis poorly defined) |
| 2 | 71–72° |
| 3 | 32–37° |
| 4 | 45–46° |

The cylinders are 4–9 mm long; the cap areas are 2.2–3.1 cm².

**Why it matters.** The cap normals set the direction of the PV jets, which drive the whole LA flow pattern, including near the device.

**Fix.** Cut each vein perpendicular to its own axis (centreline or lumen PCA, checked on the CT). Extrude a cylinder about 1–1.5× the lumen diameter along that axis. This can be scripted the same way as the mitral extension.

**Cost.** About 2–3 h, plus re-checking the 6-zone separation. All flow results change, so it must be decided before run7.

### A2. Mitral BC: keep the scaling, or use the course curve unscaled?
| option | CO | SV | peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| scaled (prepared, factor 0.9145) | 5.0 L/min | 72.9 mL | 0.86 m/s |
| unscaled course curve | 5.5 L/min | 79.8 mL | 0.95 m/s |

- Both are in the normal resting range (Kawel-Boehm 2020: men about 5.6, women about 4.5 L/min).
- **Unscaled:** the boundary condition is exactly the course's. The 4–6 cm² requirement is probably there so that the course curve gives physiological flow, and the report no longer needs a scaling paragraph.
- **Scaled:** easier to compare with run6.
- **Recommendation: unscaled.** To do it, set `CO_TARGET` so that f = 1, or add a no-scaling switch to `scale_mitral_velocity.py`. The annulus velocity must still be added.

### A3. Which part of the valve moves (reservoir volume; review C3-6)
- Only the cap moves, as a rigid body. With the smaller cap, the volume swept by the valve motion drops from 8.4 to **5.2 mL**.
- The review already noted that the rigid wall gives almost no PV inflow in systole.
- **Option.** Make the funnel wall (z < 0) its own zone that moves rigidly with the cap. The swept volume is then the annulus area times the displacement, about 8.4 mL as in run6, and the deforming region is the LA floor above the annulus.
- **Cost.** A zone split in `setup_case_mv53.py` (separate the wall at z = 0) and one more dynamic zone. Test it with a short motion preview first.

### A4. Fold the segmentation fixes into the mesh?
- **C1-2:** subtract the device from the LA mask. It is a layer of about 1 mm at the occluded ostium.
- **C1-1:** extend the inferior veins to their first branch.
- Both are fixes to the segmentation (criterion 1). The mesh does not need them: the 1 mm is negligible for the flow, and the PV cylinders replace the vein stubs anyway.
- **Recommendation.** Fix the `.nrrd` only, and say in the report that the mesh is built from the same LA. If A1 is done, the new cuts should be placed on the extended veins.
